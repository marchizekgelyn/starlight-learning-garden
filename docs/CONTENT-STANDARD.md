# Content Standard

## Child privacy

Do not add child names, birthdays, photographs, school details, location, contact information, health information, credentials, or personally identifying progress reports to the repository.

## Little Learner lessons

- Use one instruction at a time.
- Keep the primary action visible without scrolling.
- Use large controls and concrete language.
- Read the instruction aloud when requested.
- Respond to mistakes with a neutral retry cue.
- Keep sessions short and adult-guided.

## Explorer lessons

- State the mission and learning objective.
- Demonstrate the idea with a model or worked example.
- Include active practice.
- Ask the learner to explain how she knows.
- Provide corrective feedback, not only a score.
- Separate verified facts from stories, simulations, and hypotheses.

## Source quality

For science, history, health, civics, and other factual subjects, record the source, publication date, date reviewed, intended age range, and any limitations. Medical or safety-critical material must remain educational and must direct real emergencies to qualified adults and emergency services.
