# Starlight content source map

The Discovery Library is a child-safe adaptation of four educational sections from `MLavoie6927/MLavoie6927.github.io`.

| Starlight subject | Portfolio source | Child-facing treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Science | `/science/` | Observation, fair tests, variables, evidence, and safe household investigations |
| Geology | `/geology/` | Rocks, layers, weathering, erosion, deposition, and model limits |
| Archaeology | `/archaeology/` | Artifacts, context, chronology, competing explanations, ethics, and site protection |
| Health & Medical | `/medical/` | Body basics, prevention, trusted-adult help, symptoms versus diagnosis, and medicine safety boundaries |

Excluded: adult navigation, recruiting content, personal details, medication combinations, diagnosis, dosing, self-treatment, graphic material, external child-mode links, site disturbance, and unsafe activities.
