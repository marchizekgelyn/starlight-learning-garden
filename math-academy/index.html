Financial mathematics assumptions

Read complete interest guide ↗

These computations use nominal fixed rates, periodic compounding and end-of-period payments unless otherwise labeled. Real lender statements can differ because of rounding conventions, daily accrual, leap years, variable rates, origination fees, taxes, insurance, prepayment terms or regulation. The calculator does not retrieve market rates and does not provide individualized financial advice.

For loans, the default is a 12-payment-per-year amortizing structure; extra payments are applied to principal every period, and the final payment is reduced as needed. Verify offers against written lender disclosures.

Math Academy keyboard guide

Scientific calculator

Click the expression box and type a calculation, or use the on-screen keypad. Press Enter or = to evaluate. Esc clears the box. Use the RAD/DEG toggle before trigonometry.

Use ^ for exponents, pi for π, sqrt(25) for square roots, 3! for factorials, and log(32,2) for base-two logarithms.

Advanced tools

Choose a method such as Derivative or Integral. Click the first field, then press Tab to move to the next field. Press Shift + Tab to move backward. Press Enter to calculate once fields are complete.

Some tools use numerical estimates. Symbolic differentiation supports a defined subset of differentiable expressions; polynomial antiderivatives are exact within their supported class.

Practice and navigation

In Practice, press Enter to check your answer. Click New question to continue. Press / outside text fields to search lessons, or Ctrl + K to open the lesson search.

Graphing

Write one function of x per line and click Plot functions. Drag the canvas to pan; use mouse wheel or Zoom buttons to change scale. Discontinuous or undefined points may not plot.
