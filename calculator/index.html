Numerical accuracy & limitations

ATLAS supports a broad range of common school, university, engineering, financial and analytical calculations, but no calculator can guarantee an answer to every mathematical question. Symbolic calculus covers specified elementary functions and polynomial antiderivatives; numerical methods are approximations. Floating-point results can exhibit rounding and instability near singularities. Financial examples assume fixed rates, time conventions and user-supplied estimates.

The app does not require a login, internet connection, external service, or financial API. Calculation history is stored in your own browser and can be cleared or exported.
